Sizing Guide

Find your fit.

Vintxge Golf garments are designed with an athletic fit in mind, cut for movement on the course without excess. Sizing follows UK standard measurements and aligns closely with leading performance golf brands. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up.

All bespoke and commission orders are produced to your specified measurements. For any made-to-order enquiry, please include your chest, waist and height when submitting your brief and we will advise accordingly.

How to measure

  • ChestMeasure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level and parallel to the ground. Arms relaxed at your sides.
  • WaistMeasure around your natural waistline, approximately one inch above your navel. Keep the tape comfortably snug.
  • Inside LegMeasure from the top of your inner thigh down to the ankle bone. Best done standing straight.
  • HeightStand without shoes, feet together, and measure from the floor to the top of your head.
