Bespoke Design

Crafted to your specification.

No catalogue. No fixed price. No standard lead time. Every bespoke piece is produced to your specification, from the initial brief through to delivery.

How It Works
i.

Share your brief: the garment, colours, embroidery and quantity you have in mind.

ii.

We reply personally to refine the design and confirm a quotation and timings.

iii.

Your piece is produced to order, with sign off at each stage.

iv.

Delivered to you in the United Kingdom or shipped internationally.

Bespoke pieces carry a premium over our standard range, reflecting the design work and smaller production runs involved.
