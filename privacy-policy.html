Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Vintxge Golf is a golf apparel brand based in the United Kingdom. For any privacy question, contact us at vintxgegolf@outlook.com.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry or a VX Members application we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to respond to your enquiry or application. We will never sell or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

Storage

Submissions are processed via Formspree (GDPR compliant) and delivered to our team inbox, after a Google reCAPTCHA check to block spam. No data is stored on this website.

Cookies

We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. Our enquiry forms use Google reCAPTCHA to protect against spam. reCAPTCHA sets cookies and collects technical information such as your IP address that are strictly necessary for this security check, and its use is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. When you dismiss our cookie notice, that choice is saved in your browser so the notice is not shown again.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete any data we hold about you. Email vintxgegolf@outlook.com to exercise these rights.
